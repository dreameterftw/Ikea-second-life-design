# Ikea-second-life-design

Second Life — IKEA India Peer-to-Peer Resale Marketplace

Track: Sustainability Concept: A peer-to-peer secondhand furniture resale & swap marketplace, built inside the existing IKEA app.

The Idea

IKEA already runs a real Buy Back & Resell program — bring furniture to store, get a graded refund-card credit, they resell it. Second Life is that program's little sibling: instead of you → IKEA, it's you → your neighbour, with IKEA providing the trust rails in between (authenticity verification, condition standards, and safe store pickup) rather than reinventing peer-to-peer resale from scratch the way OLX or Facebook Marketplace do.

What's Different From OLX / Facebook Marketplace
Scan-to-list — scan the item's IKEA tag or pull from purchase history to auto-fill product name, real dimensions, and the original manual. No typed-in guesswork, no fake listings.
Reused condition tiers — "As New / Very Good / Well Loved," the same language IKEA's real Buy Back program already uses, instead of an invented star-rating system.
Verified Original Owner badge — automatically granted when a listing matches a real purchase on the seller's IKEA Family account.
Pickup at IKEA stores — a safe, neutral handoff point neither OLX nor Facebook Marketplace can offer, since neither has physical infrastructure.

Who This Is For
Movers — fast, low-friction listing before relocating.
Students — budget-first, verified-real, correctly sized for small rooms.
Eco-conscious buyers — circularity and impact framing, not just discount framing.
Accessibility
Minimum 44×44px tap targets throughout.
No trust/status signal relies on color alone — every badge carries a text label.
16px minimum body text, respecting the OS's existing text-scaling settings.
Every camera-based step (tag scan, guided photos) has an equally prominent manual-entry fallback.
